About MAGIZH 2026

MAGIZH 2026 is a National Level Symposium organized by the MGR YUVA Chapter of Dr. M.G.R. Educational and Research Institute, Chennai. The symposium serves as a vibrant platform where students from various disciplines come together to showcase their technical expertise, creativity, innovation, and leadership skills.

The event aims to foster collaboration among aspiring professionals, encourage innovative thinking, and provide participants with opportunities to engage in meaningful learning experiences through technical and non-technical competitions. MAGIZH 2026 is designed to inspire young minds to transform ideas into impactful solutions while building valuable connections with peers and industry enthusiasts.

Featuring a diverse range of events including Paper Presentation, Poster Designing, Shark Tank, Adaptune, Solo Singing, Group Dance, and Face Painting, the symposium celebrates both intellectual excellence and artistic talent. Participants will have the opportunity to compete, learn, network, and gain recognition at a national level.

Join us on 22 June 2026 for a day filled with innovation, creativity, competition, and unforgettable experiences as we come together to make MAGIZH 2026 a grand success.
